How CivicFix Works

A simple, transparent 3-step loop between citizens and city maintenance teams.

1

Snap & Report

Take a clear photo of the problem, write a brief description, and provide the location landmark.

2

Authorities Triage

The municipal admin department reviews your report, prioritizes urgency, and assigns an on-field repair crew.

3

Track & Resolve

Monitor live progress updates from "Submitted" to "In Progress" until the issue is verified and marked "Resolved".
